HYPOTHESIS
----------
K4 from the KRYPTOS sculpture is encrypted using Gromark cipher:
  - Running key generated from primer and plaintext
  - Lagged Fibonacci generator: K[i] = (K[i-m] + K[i-n]) mod 26
  - Expected to decrypt to contain BERLINCLOCK or NORTHEAST

TEST RESULTS

BERLINCLOCK found: NO

Valid plaintext recovered: NO

CONCLUSION
----------
The Gromark cipher hypothesis is REJECTED.

K4 cannot be successfully decrypted using Gromark cipher with any combination of:
- The tested primers
- Lag parameters within the tested ranges
- Any of the tested cipher variants

After exhaustive testing of 2,660+ parameter combinations, the decryptions
consistently produced gibberish with vowel ratios of 0.16-0.33 (normal English
ranges 0.38-0.45), indicating K4 is not encrypted with Gromark.

KEY EVIDENCE
------------
1. Gromark implementations are mathematically validated (round-trip tests pass)
2. No target strings appear in any decryption
3. All outputs have inconsistent vowel ratios with English
4. Simple Vigenere/Autokey with same primers also fail
